❓ Common Questions

Why can't board ads be clicked?
Board displays are TVs/projectors at the pool table - there's no touchscreen. We value them based on impressions and estimated viewers (typically 2-4 people watching per impression).
How do you track impressions?
Every time your ad rotates into view on a board or loads in the player app, we log an impression with bar ID, time, and context. You get full transparency in your dashboard.
What creative formats do you accept?
Board ads: 1920×1080 images (PNG, JPG). In-app banners: 320×100. We recommend high-contrast designs that read well in bar lighting.
Is there an approval process?
Yes, all campaigns are reviewed before going live. We typically approve within 24 hours. We don't allow competitor ads at bars with exclusivity deals.
